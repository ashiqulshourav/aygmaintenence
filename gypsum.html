Premium Gypsum Partition & False Ceiling Solutions in UAE

Professional Gypsum Partition Solutions in United Arab Emirates —

Transforming an open space into functional rooms requires precision and high-quality materials. At AYG Maintenance, we specialize in professional gypsum partition installations that cater to the fast-paced lifestyle of the UAE. Whether you are looking to create a private home office, separate a large living area, or build dedicated cabins in a commercial workspace, our gypsum solutions offer the perfect blend of durability and aesthetics.

Gypsum boards, commonly known as drywall, are the preferred choice in Dubai’s construction industry due to their lightweight nature and structural flexibility. Unlike traditional brick walls, gypsum partitions are "dry construction," meaning they are installed quickly without the mess of cement and mortar. Our team ensures that every partition is perfectly aligned using high-grade galvanized iron (GI) studs and tracks, providing a sturdy foundation that meets UAE building safety standards. We take pride in delivering spaces that are not just divided, but enhanced.

Premium False Ceiling & Decorative Gypsum Designs

A ceiling is more than just a cover; it is a canvas for interior design. In the UAE, modern gypsum ceilings have become a signature feature of luxury villas and high-end apartments. AYG Maintenance provides comprehensive ceiling services, from minimalist flat designs to complex multi-layered tray ceilings with integrated LED cove lighting. Our designs add depth, character, and a sense of luxury to any room while hiding unsightly electrical wiring and AC ductwork.

Our expert craftsmen are skilled in creating geometric patterns, curved arches, and decorative bulkheads that reflect the contemporary architectural trends seen in Dubai and Abu Dhabi. Beyond aesthetics, these ceilings play a crucial role in thermal regulation. By adding a layer of insulation, gypsum ceilings help keep indoor temperatures stable, significantly reducing energy consumption and cooling costs during the intense summer months. We ensure a flawless finish that is ready for premium painting, giving your home or office a sophisticated, gallery-like feel.

Specialized Fire-Rated & Moisture-Resistant Board Installation

Safety and longevity are non-negotiable in the UAE’s harsh climate and strict regulatory environment. AYG Maintenance utilizes specialized gypsum boards tailored for specific environments. For commercial projects, high-rise buildings, and kitchens, we install Fire-Rated (Type X) Gypsum Boards. These boards contain glass fibers and other non-combustible materials that act as a barrier to heat, providing vital protection and meeting Civil Defence safety requirements.

In areas prone to humidity, such as bathrooms, laundry rooms, and pantries, we use Moisture-Resistant (Green) Boards. These are specifically treated to prevent mold growth and water damage, ensuring your walls remain structurally sound for years to come. By choosing the right material for each zone, we protect your investment from the inside out. Our technical team evaluates each site to recommend the best board density and thickness (typically 12.5mm to 15mm), ensuring your project is compliant, safe, and built to last.

Acoustic Insulation for Quiet & Private Spaces

In a bustling city like Dubai, acoustic privacy is a luxury. AYG Maintenance provides advanced soundproofing solutions using high-density acoustic gypsum boards and rockwool insulation. This service is ideal for corporate boardrooms where confidentiality is key, or for residential bedrooms and home theaters where you want to block out external noise. Our partitions are designed to reduce decibel levels and prevent sound leakage through the walls and ceilings.

We don't just stop at the board; we pay close attention to the "weak points" like joints, electrical outlets, and door frames. By using acoustic sealants and multi-layered board techniques, we create a sound barrier that rivals traditional masonry. Our goal is to provide you with a peaceful environment where you can work or relax without the intrusion of city traffic or neighbor noise. With our acoustic solutions, your indoor space becomes a sanctuary of calm and productivity.

Seamless Finishing, Jointing, and Repair Services

The difference between a DIY job and a professional AYG Maintenance installation is the finish. A common problem with gypsum walls in the UAE is cracking at the joints over time. We prevent this by using a rigorous three-coat jointing process. We apply high-quality joint tape and premium compound (mudding), feathering the edges meticulously to ensure that when the wall is painted, it looks like one solid, continuous surface.

In addition to new installations, we offer expert Gypsum Repair Services. Whether it is a hole from a door handle, water damage from a leaking AC, or settling cracks, our team can patch and restore your walls to their original condition. We match the existing texture and finish so perfectly that the repair is invisible. We understand that a smooth, crack-free surface is essential for the high-end look that Dubai properties demand, and we deliver that perfection every time.
