Gypsum Partition

Premium Gypsum Partition & False Ceiling Solutions in UAE

Professional Gypsum Partition Solutions in United Arab Emirates —

Transforming an open space into functional rooms requires precision and high-quality materials. At AYG Maintenance, we specialize in professional gypsum partition installations that cater to the fast-paced lifestyle of the UAE. Whether you are looking to create a private home office, separate a large living area, or build dedicated cabins in a commercial workspace, our gypsum solutions offer the perfect blend of durability and aesthetics.

Gypsum boards, commonly known as drywall, are the preferred choice in Dubai’s construction industry due to their lightweight nature and structural flexibility. Unlike traditional brick walls, gypsum partitions are "dry construction," meaning they are installed quickly without the mess of cement and mortar. Our team ensures that every partition is perfectly aligned using high-grade galvanized iron (GI) studs and tracks, providing a sturdy foundation that meets UAE building safety standards. We take pride in delivering spaces that are not just divided, but enhanced.

Premium False Ceiling & Decorative Gypsum Designs

A ceiling is more than just a cover; it is a canvas for interior design. In the UAE, modern gypsum ceilings have become a signature feature of luxury villas and high-end apartments. AYG Maintenance provides comprehensive ceiling services, from minimalist flat designs to complex multi-layered tray ceilings with integrated LED cove lighting. Our designs add depth, character, and a sense of luxury to any room while hiding unsightly electrical wiring and AC ductwork.

Our expert craftsmen are skilled in creating geometric patterns, curved arches, and decorative bulkheads that reflect the contemporary architectural trends seen in Dubai and Abu Dhabi. Beyond aesthetics, these ceilings play a crucial role in thermal regulation. By adding a layer of insulation, gypsum ceilings help keep indoor temperatures stable, significantly reducing energy consumption and cooling costs during the intense summer months. We ensure a flawless finish that is ready for premium painting, giving your home or office a sophisticated, gallery-like feel.
