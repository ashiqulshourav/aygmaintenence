Frquently Asked Questions

What services does AYG Maintenance provide?

We offer a comprehensive range of professional maintenance solutions across Dubai and Abu Dhabi, including deep cleaning, gypsum partition and ceiling installation, interior and exterior painting, and expert floor and wall tiling for both residential and commercial properties.

How long does a typical gypsum ceiling or partition installation take?

The timeline depends on the complexity and size of the project. A standard room's gypsum work usually takes 2–4 days, including installation and finishing. We provide a specific timeframe after an initial site inspection to ensure minimal disruption to your routine.

Do you provide eco-friendly cleaning services?

Yes, we prioritize your health and the environment. Our cleaning team uses high-quality, eco-friendly, and non-toxic products that are safe for your family, pets, and employees while ensuring a deep and hygienic clean.

Can I customize my painting or tiling service?

Absolutely. We offer fully customizable solutions. Whether you need a specific color palette for your walls or a unique pattern for your tiling, our experts work closely with you to match your aesthetic preferences and functional requirements.

Are your maintenance services available for both homes and offices?

Yes, AYG Maintenance serves both residential (villas and apartments) and commercial (offices, retail shops, and warehouses) sectors. We tailor our approach to meet the specific standards and schedules required for each type of space.

How do I get a quote for my maintenance project?

You can request a free quote by contacting us through our call center +971 52 351 5200 or +971 54 747 6153. For services like tiling or gypsum work, we recommend a brief site visit so we can provide a transparent and accurate cost estimate based on your specific needs.

Why should I choose AYG Maintenance over other providers in the UAE?

With over 7 years of experience in the UAE market, we combine local expertise with professional craftsmanship. We are known for our reliability, attention to detail, and commitment to delivering high-quality results at competitive prices.

Do you offer emergency or same-day cleaning services?

We understand that sometimes maintenance needs are urgent. Depending on our team's availability, we offer same-day cleaning and minor repair services across Dubai and Abu Dhabi to help you maintain a safe and comfortable environment.
